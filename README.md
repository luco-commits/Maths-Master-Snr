# MathsMaster Secondary

GitHub Pages ready PWA for Queensland secondary mathematics, Years 7 to 12.

Years 7 to 10 are structured around Australian Curriculum v9.0 content strands used in Queensland. Years 11 and 12 provide Essential Mathematics, General Mathematics, Mathematical Methods and Specialist Mathematics pathways based on the QCAA 2025 senior syllabuses used for students completing in 2026 or beyond.

## Publish
Upload all contents to the root of a GitHub repository. In Settings > Pages, deploy the main branch from /(root).

## Update caching
Increment CACHE_NAME in sw.js after future changes.
